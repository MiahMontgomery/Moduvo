Moduvo Platform – UI/UX Design Direction Memo

Color Scheme: The Moduvo platform will use the previously agreed-upon color scheme combining a clean neutral base with a rich accent. Specifically, an off-white or light ivory background and charcoal-black text provide a modern, high-contrast canvas, while a refined gold tone serves as the primary accent color. This palette was chosen for its strong commercial appeal and its alignment with luxury modular storage systems – the neutral tones feel contemporary and upscale, and the gold accents convey a sense of luxury that draws attention to key interactive elements (such as call-to-action buttons and price highlights). The overall effect should be an interface that appears elegant and inviting, reflecting the high-end nature of Moduvo’s furniture line without sacrificing user-friendly clarity.

Revised User Flow & Email Capture: The user experience should follow a clear sequence that delays email capture until after the product visualization is shown. The journey will proceed as follows:
	1.	Product Configuration: The user selects and customizes a product (choosing the model, finish, and any modular components or add-ons).
	2.	Visualization: Once the configuration is complete, the platform displays an interactive 3D/AR rendering of the customized product in the user’s space. This gives the customer a tangible preview of the design.
	3.	Quote Form (Email Capture): After the rendering is visible and the user has engaged with it, the system prompts the user with a quote request form. This form should ask for the user’s email address and any other necessary info only at this stage, not before.
	4.	Estimate Delivery: Upon submission of the form, the application will confirm the request and email the detailed price estimate directly to the user.

This adjusted flow ensures the user first experiences the product visualization (increasing confidence and engagement) before being asked to provide contact information. It aligns with a concierge-like sales approach, respecting the user’s time and interest by showing value (the rendered design and live quote) prior to any email sign-up.

Brand Tone & UX Consistency: Throughout the interface and flow, maintain Moduvo’s refined brand tone and high-end UX style. All text and prompts should be polished yet approachable – for example, use phrasing like “Visualize Your Space” or “Request Full Quote” to sound helpful and professional, in line with a luxury showroom experience. Visual cues and interactions should be subtle and elegant: highlight the gold accent on active buttons or selected options, use smooth transitions when loading the AR viewer or quote form, and provide gentle feedback (e.g. a simple checkmark or brief success message) when the quote request is submitted. The design should remain clean and minimalist with ample white space, letting the product imagery and materials shine. By following these guidelines, the platform’s UX will feel intuitive and premium, reinforcing Moduvo’s image as a luxury modular furniture platform while ensuring users have a seamless, enjoyable journey from configuration to quote.
